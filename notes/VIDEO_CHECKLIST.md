# Demo video (max 2 minutes). Order matters.

Before recording: phone in English, light mode, Android 11 emulator (or dynamicColor = false). Do NOT rotate before the filter step: rotation rebuilds the list.

1. Open an expense (tap a row). Say D6: hero amount + info rows. Show all five fields.
2. Back (arrow), then press the + button: empty form. Say D7 (chip row) and D8 (bottom Save button).
3. Type an amount, then clear it / type 0 -> the error slides in, Save is gray. Fix it, pick KHR or USD, choose a category, pick a date from the calendar. Save.
4. Show the "Expense saved" snackbar and the total COUNTING UP (say it counts in KHR; USD uses 4,000).
5. Open an expense > Edit > change something > Save. Show the total changing.
6. (Optional) change a field and press Back -> "Discard changes?" -> Keep editing / Discard.
7. Open an expense > Delete > dialog > Confirm. Show you are back on the Dashboard.
8. Say D9 (dropdown). Choose a category: list and total change. Rotate the phone with the filter on: the category stays.
9. (Optional, 10 sec) Accessibility > Remove animations: total changes at once.
